# Project Constraints Essay
## Social Calendar Browser Extension
### Security
It is imperative that our proposed browser extension has airtight security. Google Calendar is a widely used application; it has workplace, school, and personal uses. There needs to be a private/hidden feature within our browser extension to hide certain events from others (take, for instance, scheduling a job interview and your co-worker sees it via our extension). We cannot risk leaking personal events and data to others.
