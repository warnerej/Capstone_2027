### Communication 

Discord is the main method of communication. This allows us to message each other along with Dr. Aurisano, our advisor, via the same channel of communication. Texting/calling via phone will be used as a backup method to reach a team member quicker. 

Team members are expected to respond to Discord messages within 12 hours on weekdays, with a cutoff of 7pm Friday evening. Team members are not required to respond over the weekend, but it is encouraged that they at least read important messages and respond if necessary. This is subject to change as the team nears large deadlines, where additional effort and communication may be required. 

Team-only meetings will be held between Nate, Elliot, and William on a weekly basis, with 3:30pm on Thursdays being the default meeting time. This can be rescheduled as needed. Team-only meetings will be used to individually communicate quick updates, roadblocks, and future goals, as well as check in on the team’s progress against their timeline. Advisor meetings including Dr. Aurisano will be held on a bi-weekly basis on Tuesdays at 3:30pm, but again can be rescheduled as needed. Advisor meetings will focus more on the team’s progress, validation of current work, scope discussions, and more. 

### Decision-Making Process 

Decision-making will follow a simple flow. First, any team member can propose ideas towards the decision. Once proposed, the team members will discuss said idea and flesh out pros and cons. If the team cannot come to a conclusive decision regarding said idea, the tie-breaker will fall to the input of the team member who is most responsible for the decision. For example, if a consensus cannot be reached on which testing framework to use, the tie-breaker will fall to William, since he is leading the QA responsibilities. 

### Conflict-Resolution Plan 

If conflict arises, it is expected that the conflict remains between those initially involved unless an agreement cannot be reached. Bloating conflict wastes valuable time and pulls other team members/the advisor into often small disagreements. If a conclusion cannot be reached initially, those involved will bring it to the entire team during the weekly meeting and each communicate their stance. If the conflict remains unresolved, the team will collectively take the conflict to the advisor for their input. As a last resort, the instructor will be called to action to be involved in handling the conflict.
